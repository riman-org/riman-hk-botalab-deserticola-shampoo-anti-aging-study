## English overview

Background: The global shift towards clean beauty has intensified demand for non-irritating, botanical anti-aging solutions for the scalp, particularly in humid subtropical climates like Hong Kong. Methods: A stratified survey of 200 consumers (N=200) evaluated the BOTALAB Deserticola Shampoo, focusing on its patented Deserticola/Chlorella Ferment (Loliolide), Inonotus Obliquus (Chaga), and Centella Asiatica formulation against traditional retinol-based products. Results: The product demonstrated exceptional sensory acceptance, with a Top-2-Box score of 95.5% for lightweight texture suitability in HK's humidity. However, efficacy trust remains moderate; only 44.5% strongly agreed that the Deserticola patent provides significant anti-aging benefits, mirroring skepticism toward Chaga (45.0%)
